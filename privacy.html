What information does the Application collect?

The Application has no accounts and no registration. It does not ask you for your name, email address, date of birth, or any similar personal detail, and the Service Provider does not collect any of these from you.

The Application does ask for your age, as described in the Your age section below. You can choose "Prefer not to say" instead.

Everything the Application creates while you use it, including your age answer, coin balance, streaks, focus session history, chosen subjects and the blocks you own, is stored only on your own device. It is never sent to the Service Provider, and the Service Provider has no way to see it.

The Service Provider does not send marketing communications and operates no mailing list.

There is one exception to all of the above. If you choose to email the Service Provider, your email address and whatever you write are held for as long as it takes to deal with your message, and are deleted once the matter is resolved. They are not used for anything else.

Advertising

The Application shows adverts supplied by Google AdMob. To do that, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects information from your device, including:

  • your advertising ID (the Android advertising ID, or on iOS the Identifier for Advertisers, if you allow it)
  • device information, such as model and operating system version
  • an approximate location derived from your IP address

This information is collected by Google through the Mobile Ads SDK included in the Application. The Service Provider does not receive or store it. Your advertising ID is a unique identifier and is not anonymous.

On iOS, the advertising ID is only available if you allow it when iOS asks whether the Application may track you. The Application only asks this of people who have said they are 18 or over. If you choose not to allow it, or are never asked, adverts are still shown, but without that identifier. You can change this at any time in your iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

Where the law requires it, the Application asks for your consent before this information is used for personalised advertising. You can change that choice at any time using the privacy option on the More page inside the Application. Google's handling of this information is governed by Google's own privacy policy, linked below.

Google may share this information with the other advertising technology companies that take part in its advert auctions. Where the law requires it, these companies are listed in the consent form, and you can see and change your choices for them there. The Application does not use any other advertising networks, and apart from Google and these companies, no third party receives information from the Application.

Your age

The first time you open the Application, it asks how old you are. Its only purpose is to make sure the adverts you see are suitable for your age. You can choose "Prefer not to say", and the Application works exactly the same either way.

Your answer is stored only on your own device. The number itself is never sent to the Service Provider or to Google. What the Application does with it:

  • Under 13: adverts are never personalised, and each advert request tells Google to apply its child protections.
  • 13 to 17: adverts are never personalised, and each advert request tells Google to apply its teen protections.
  • Prefer not to say: adverts are never personalised.
  • 18 or over: adverts may be personalised, but only if you also agree to it in the consent form.

So while Google never learns your exact age, it does learn that a request comes from someone under 13 or someone aged 13 to 17, when you have told the Application so.

The age question is always asked before the consent form is shown. Consent for personalised advertising is never relied on for anyone who says they are under 18: whatever they choose in the consent form, their adverts are not personalised. For anyone who says they are under 13, Google's consent system is also told that they are under the age at which they can give consent for themselves.

If you said you were under 18, the Application asks again once a year, so that your adverts can change when you turn 18. It does not ask again if you said 18 or over, or chose "Prefer not to say".

To change your answer at any other time, clear the Application's storage as described in the Deleting your data section. You will be asked again the next time you open it. Note that this also deletes your progress.

Legal basis for processing

Where the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR apply, the Service Provider relies on:

  • Consent for the use of device identifiers, including your advertising ID, for advertising. Consent is collected through the form shown inside the Application. You may withdraw it at any time, which does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Legitimate interests for using your age answer to make sure adverts are suitable for your age, including telling Google when a request comes from someone under 13 or aged 13 to 17. This protects children and teenagers, as the UK Age Appropriate Design Code expects.
  • Legitimate interests where you email the Service Provider, so that it can read and reply to your message.
  • Legal obligation where processing is required to comply with the law.

If you do not give consent, Google may still show limited adverts that do not use device identifiers and are not personalised.

Cookies and similar technologies

The Application does not use cookies. Google's Mobile Ads SDK uses device identifiers, including your advertising ID, to deliver and measure advertising. Where required by law, your consent is requested before these are used for advertising.

Automated decision making and profiling

The Service Provider does not carry out automated decision making that produces legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affects you.

Personalised advertising involves profiling carried out by Google. It is only ever used if you have said you are 18 or over and have agreed to it in the consent form. You can decline it, or change your mind later, using the privacy option on the More page.

Does the Application collect precise location?

No. The Application requests no location permission and does not access your device's location. The approximate location described in the Advertising section is derived by Google from your IP address and is not precise location data.

Does the Application use artificial intelligence?

No. The Application does not use artificial intelligence to process your data or to provide any of its features.

Data retention

The Service Provider operates no servers and stores no personal data about you.

  • Data created by the Application is held only on your device, for as long as the Application is installed.
  • Information collected by Google is retained by Google according to Google's own privacy policy.
  • Emails you send to the Service Provider are kept only until your query has been dealt with, and are then deleted.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored on your own device, you can delete all of it yourself at any time, without contacting anyone:

  • uninstall the Application, or
  • open your device settings, find Focoblocks, and clear its storage

Either removes all Application data from your device permanently, including your age answer.

To reset or delete your advertising ID on Android, use the Privacy and Ads section of your device settings. On iOS, you can stop apps using it under Privacy & Security, then Tracking. To withdraw your advertising consent, use the privacy option on the More page inside the Application.

If you have any questions about deleting your data, contact the Service Provider at focoblocks@proton.me.

Children's privacy

The Application is not intended for children under 13 years of age, and the Service Provider does not market it to them. Because children may still use it, the Application asks every user's age and protects anyone under 18, as described in the Your age section.

For anyone who says they are under 13, adverts are never personalised and Google is told to apply its child protections to every advert request. For anyone who says they are 13 to 17, adverts are never personalised and Google is told to apply its teen protections.

The Service Provider collects no personal information from anyone directly, including children. Age answers stay on the device. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern about your child's use of the Application, please contact the Service Provider at focoblocks@proton.me.
