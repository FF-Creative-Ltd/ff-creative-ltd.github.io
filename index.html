Focoblocks

Focus to earn coins, then spend them on blocks and pets for your shelves.

Focoblocks is a focus timer for studying and work. Start a session and earn coins for the time you stay focused. Spend them in the shop on blocks to fill your shelves, and on pets that come to visit. Focus each day and your blocks keep earning coins for you too.
